A receipt for the day: weather, your Tap Log streaks, today's shared hour with friends and a prompt to make something. Drawn in pure black and white at thermal-printer width.

Paper
On the slip

Most USB and Bluetooth receipt printers show up as normal printers: Print sends the slip at true width. For printers driven by a phone app, save the PNG; it's already 1-bit at the printer's 203 dpi. Streaks and the shared hour come from Tap Log and Shared Hour in this browser. Weather by Open-Meteo.
