Six of your X bookmarks as an eight-page zine, printed on one sheet, one cut, folded by hand. Pages are set by the Poster Machine.

Posts

    Folding

    1. Print single-sided, landscape, at 100% (no "fit to page").
    2. Fold in half the long way, then in quarters the short way; unfold.
    3. Fold in half the long way again, printed side out, and cut along the solid line from the fold to the middle.
    4. Open it, fold the long way again, and push the two ends together so the cut opens into a diamond.
    5. Fold flat into a booklet with the cover on top.
