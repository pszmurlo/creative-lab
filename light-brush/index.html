Walking
  1. Dark room or night. Camera on a tripod, manual focus on where you'll walk.
  2. Shutter = sweep time + 2 s, ISO 100, f/8. Start the exposure, then press Run.
  3. Phone screen to full brightness, auto-brightness off. Hold it upright, screen to the camera.
  4. Walk sideways at a steady pace for the whole sweep. A beep counts you in.
