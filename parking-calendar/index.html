Parking
calendar.

Twelve months of perfect parking. Each month's drawing is seeded by its date, so a year always comes out the same, and every page is a plotter-ready SVG.

Printing saves all twelve pages as one PDF at true size. For a pen plotter, use the SVGs: one layer per pen, sized in millimetres. The month names and dates are text, so convert them to single-line strokes first (Inkscape's Hershey Text extension) or plot them as outlines.
