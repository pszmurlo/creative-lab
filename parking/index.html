Perfect
parking.

Plotter drawings of parallel parking. Each car reverses in on full right lock, then full left lock, the two-arc move from the maths of perfect parking. Grey ghosts show the body, red lines trace its corners.

Layout
Paper

The SVG is sized in millimetres with one layer per pen, ready for Inkscape, AxiDraw or vpype. Inspired by Simon Blackburn's "The geometry of perfect parking"; the gaps shown here come from this simulation, not his formula.
